Guide

1. What is AQI? +
AQI (Air Quality Index) is a measure used to indicate how clean or polluted the air is. Lower AQI values indicate better air quality, while higher values indicate greater health risks.
2. What does PM2.5 mean? +
PM2.5 refers to tiny particulate matter with a diameter of less than 2.5 micrometers. These particles can enter deep into the lungs and may cause respiratory and cardiovascular problems.
3. What is PM10? +
PM10 consists of larger dust, pollen, and smoke particles with a diameter less than 10 micrometers. High levels can irritate the eyes, nose, and throat.
4. What is NO₂? +
Nitrogen Dioxide (NO₂) is a harmful gas mainly produced by vehicles, power plants, and industrial activities. High exposure can affect lung function and worsen asthma.
5. What is CO? +
Carbon Monoxide (CO) is a colorless and odorless gas produced by incomplete combustion of fuels. High concentrations can be dangerous to human health.
6. How is AQI calculated? +
AQI is calculated using the concentration of pollutants such as PM2.5, PM10, NO₂, CO, SO₂, and O₃. These values are converted into a standardized index.
7. What AQI level is considered safe? +
AQI 0-50 : Good
AQI 51-100 : Fair
AQI 101-150 : Moderate
AQI 151-200 : Poor
AQI 201-300 : Very Poor
AQI >300 : Very Poor
8. Why is monitoring air quality important? +
Monitoring air quality helps people make informed decisions about outdoor activities and helps reduce health risks caused by air pollution.
9. How does this dashboard help users? +
This dashboard provides real-time information about AQI, weather conditions, pollutant levels, health recommendations, and location-based air quality visualization.
10. What is the objective of the Plasma Air Interaction Project? +
The project aims to study the interaction between plasma generated by a Tesla coil and surrounding air, while monitoring environmental parameters and visualizing air quality data through a web dashboard.

About

Project Overview

The Air Quality Dashboard is a real-time web application that enables users to monitor and visualize air quality, weather conditions, and pollutant levels across cities worldwide. It provides important environmental data such as AQI, temperature, humidity, wind speed, and pollutant concentrations through an intuitive interface. The dashboard also includes interactive charts, city comparison tools, and health recommendations to help users better understand and respond to changing air quality conditions.

Features

  • Real-time AQI and pollutant data (SO₂, PM2.5, PM10, NO₂, CO).
  • Live weather conditions including temperature, humidity, and wind speed.
  • Interactive Leaflet.js map with location markers.
  • Side-by-side city comparison with bar charts.
  • Personalized health recommendations based on AQI level.
  • FAQ guide on air quality concepts.

Built With

- HTML5 - CSS3 - JavaScript (ES Modules) - Vite - Leaflet.js - Chart.js - OpenWeatherMap API - Open-Meteo API

Data Sources

Weather data is sourced from the OpenWeatherMap API. Air quality and pollutant data is sourced from the Open-Meteo Air Quality API. Data accuracy depends on these third-party providers and may vary by location.
